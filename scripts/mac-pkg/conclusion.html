Open Unhook to finish setup

@DEVELOPMENT_WARNING@
  1. Open Unhook from Applications as your normal user.
  2. Connect the browser companion in Setup. During the beta, follow the guided Load unpacked instructions and confirm the browser reports Connected.
  3. Wait for the package-installed system service to show Ready in Setup before enabling system blocking.
  4. Optionally enable Launch Unhook at login. Keep Unhook running for automatic schedules.

The package-installed system service handles routine blocking changes without password prompts. Updates, revoked permissions and system changes may require approval again.

Installer success confirms installation. Service readiness and browser connection are checked separately inside Unhook.
