Unhook for Mac

The installer places Unhook in Applications and a persistent system blocker in protected system locations. macOS may ask for administrator authorization to install or update the app.

@DEVELOPMENT_WARNING@

Unhook controls your focus sessions and schedules. Its browser companion blocks websites in your browser. Connect the companion in Unhook Setup after installation.

Optional system blocking uses a background service. Installer authorization loads the package-installed service. Ordinary focus and schedule transitions use that service without another password prompt.

For updates, finish focus and quit Unhook. If the previous version registered an embedded service, choose Remove system service in Setup before quitting. Your blocklist and schedules are preserved.
